About ChiffTown (Boilerplate)

ChiffTown is a browser-based virtual town where people hang out face-to-face over live video in themed venues — pubs, nightclubs, cinemas, lounges, arcades, and more. Unlike conventional video call platforms, ChiffTown is designed to feel like going somewhere: users walk into a venue, join or create a room, and socialise in a persistent, atmosphere-rich space. It's completely free, requires no download or sign-up, and supports up to 8 people per video room across 15+ themed venues. An XP and progression system rewards regular visitors with levels, achievements, coins, and access to exclusive venues.

Short version: ChiffTown is a free, browser-based virtual town with 15+ themed video chat venues — no download, no sign-up. Users hang out face-to-face in pubs, nightclubs, cinemas, and more, earning XP as they explore.

Fact Sheet

Platform
Browser-based web application (chifftown.com)
Cost
Free — no premium tier, no sign-up required
Venues
15+ themed venues including pub, nightclub, cinema, lounge, arcade, casino, wellness centre, observatory, and more
Video Capacity
Up to 8 participants per video room
Technology
WebRTC peer-to-peer video, WebSockets for real-time presence, Node.js backend
Access to Entry
~10 seconds from link to live video chat
Progression System
XP, levels, coins, gems, daily streaks, achievements, building unlocks
Target Audience
Friends wanting casual online hangouts, remote workers, long-distance relationships, social communities
Compatibility
All modern browsers (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge) — no app or plugin required
PWA
Installable as a Progressive Web App on mobile and desktop
