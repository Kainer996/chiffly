Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 20, 2026

What We Collect

ChiffTown collects minimal data to provide the service:

  • Username — chosen by you when you enter the town
  • Optional newsletter email — only if you choose to subscribe for ChiffTown updates
  • XP and progress data — stored locally in your browser (localStorage)
  • Video/audio streams — transmitted peer-to-peer via WebRTC, never recorded or stored on our servers
  • Chat messages — processed in real-time via WebSocket, not permanently stored

What We Don't Collect

  • We don't require email addresses or phone numbers to enter the town
  • We don't use tracking cookies or third-party analytics unless this policy is updated first
  • We don't sell or share any data with third parties
  • We don't record video or audio calls

Newsletter Emails

If you subscribe to ChiffTown updates, we store your email address so we can send occasional product/news updates. Newsletter emails are optional and are not required to enter the town. Each public newsletter workflow should include an unsubscribe route before broad promotion.

Local Storage

Your XP, level, achievements, and preferences are stored in your browser's localStorage. This data stays on your device and is not transmitted to our servers unless you create an account.

WebRTC

Video and audio streams are transmitted directly between participants using WebRTC peer-to-peer connections. Our server facilitates the initial connection (signaling) but does not relay or record media streams.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Google Fonts — for typography (subject to Google's privacy policy)
  • Cloudflare CDN — for serving static assets

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach out via our social channels linked on the homepage.
