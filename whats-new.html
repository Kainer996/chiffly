March 2026
Performance

Venue pages load up to 90% faster

The pub, nightclub, cinema, lounge, arcade, and adventure guild now load in a fraction of the time. We converted all large venue background images to WebP — saving megabytes on every page visit.

  • Pub background: 1.7MB → 170KB
  • Nightclub background: 1.6MB → 129KB
  • Cinema, Lounge, Arcade backgrounds: similar savings
  • Google Fonts and Font Awesome now load without blocking first paint

ChiffTown now has a full progression system. Every visit, message, and game earns you XP. Level up to unlock exclusive venues — the observatory at Level 10, the Chronos Club at Level 15, the Aetherium Lab at Level 20.

  • Welcome tutorial for new visitors

Full Accessibility Pass

ChiffTown is now usable by everyone — keyboard navigation, screen readers, and users with motion sensitivity all fully supported.

Performance

Faster Load Times, Offline Support

Major infrastructure improvements making ChiffTown feel snappy, even on slower connections.
