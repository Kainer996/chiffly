FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is ChiffTown?

ChiffTown is a free virtual town where you hang out with friends in themed venues using live video chat. Think of it as a social world where every building is a different vibe — pubs, nightclubs, cinemas, arcades, and more.

Do I need to download anything?

Nope! ChiffTown runs entirely in your browser. No downloads, no plugins, no app store. Just open the link and you're in. Works on desktop, mobile, and tablet.

Is ChiffTown free?

Yes, ChiffTown is completely free to use. All venues, video chat, text chat, and core features are free. The XP system, achievements, and progression are all included.

How does the XP and leveling system work?

You earn XP by visiting venues, chatting with people, completing daily challenges, and playing mini-games. As you level up, you unlock new buildings and earn achievements. Keep a daily streak going for bonus rewards!

How many people can be in a video chat?

Each venue supports up to 8 simultaneous video streams with real-time text chat alongside. Perfect for hanging out with your friend group.

Can I create a private room?

Yes! Use the "Start a Room" button to generate a unique invite link. Share it with friends via WhatsApp, text, or any messaging app — only people with the link can join.

What venues are available in ChiffTown?

ChiffTown has 15+ venues including The Chiff Inn (cosy pub), The Electric Nightclub, The Grand Cinema, The Lounge, Wellness Centre, Casino Royale, Adventure Guild, Arcade, Observatory, Aetherium, Chronos Bar, Fishing Dock, and Pet Shelter — with more being added regularly.

Do I need to create an account?

You can jump straight into ChiffTown as a guest — no sign-up required. Creating a free account lets you save your XP progress, unlock achievements, and build a profile that persists across sessions.

Is ChiffTown safe and moderated?

ChiffTown uses room-based privacy, so you only video chat with people in your specific room. All rooms are voluntary — nobody can add you without your knowledge. We take community safety seriously.

Can I use ChiffTown on my phone?

Yes! ChiffTown works on smartphones, tablets, laptops, and desktops — no app required. Open it in Chrome or Safari on your phone and you're good to go. Full video chat, XP, and all venues available on mobile.

How is ChiffTown different from Zoom or Google Meet?

ChiffTown is built for hanging out, not meetings. Instead of a plain video grid, you're actually inside a venue — a cosy pub, buzzing nightclub, retro arcade. You earn XP, unlock buildings, and play games. It's what video chat feels like when it's actually fun.

What browser works best with ChiffTown?

Chrome, Firefox, Edge, or Safari (v15+) all work great. You'll need camera and microphone permissions for video chat — but you can also join in text-only mode. No plugins or extensions needed.
